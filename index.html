How to play

Simple to learn, hard to master.
• Tap, click or press Space to flap.
• Fly through the gaps — each pipe is 1 point.
• Fly through the exact centre for a combo.
• Grab golden coins to unlock new birds.
• P / Esc pauses, R restarts, M mutes.
• Medals at 10 / 20 / 30 / 45 points.
• Practice mode is crash-free (no score saved).
• Progress is saved on this device.
